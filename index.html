Common questions

What brokers ask us.

What CRM formats does it support?

SpotDealers accepts any standard CSV export. If your CRM can export a spreadsheet, it works. We support ProABD, Cronetic, and generic CSV formats out of the box.

Does it send emails automatically?

No. SpotDealers writes the emails and delivers them to you for review. You send them from your own email account. Nothing goes out without your approval.

How does it find current contacts at dealerships?

SpotDealers uses contact enrichment to find the current fleet manager or used car manager at each dealership. This is critical because staff turnover at dealerships is high and your old contacts may no longer be there.

What are the buying signals it monitors?

SpotDealers monitors public inventory data including dealer lot size changes, new vehicle listings on major platforms, and auction activity patterns to identify dealers who are actively buying and will need transport soon.

Is my transaction data kept private?

Yes. Your CRM data is used only to generate your own prospecting list. It is never shared with other brokers or third parties.
